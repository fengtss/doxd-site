Doxd Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Doxd processes information while providing its Discord bot features.

1. Information Doxd May Process

Depending on the features being used, Doxd may process information provided by Discord, including:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server IDs
  • Channel and role IDs
  • Usernames and display names
  • Server configuration and bot settings
  • Moderation records and command usage
  • Message content when required for commands, moderation, filters, automations, or other enabled features

2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only as necessary to operate Doxd, execute commands, provide moderation and automation features, maintain server settings, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and improve reliability.

3. Data Storage

Some information may be stored when necessary for persistent features such as server settings, moderation records, custom configurations, logging, or automated features.

Data that is no longer needed may be deleted or removed during routine maintenance.

4. Message Content

Doxd may access message content when required for features such as prefix commands, moderation filters, autoresponders, logging, or other message-based functionality.

Doxd does not sell message content or personal information.

5. Sharing of Information

Doxd does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be disclosed when required by law, necessary to protect the service, or necessary to investigate abuse or security incidents.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

7. Data Removal

Server owners or users may request deletion of applicable stored data by contacting the Doxd developers.

8. Third-Party Services

Doxd operates through Discord and may rely on hosting, database, or infrastructure providers required to operate the service. Use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own privacy policies and terms.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Doxd changes. The date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.
